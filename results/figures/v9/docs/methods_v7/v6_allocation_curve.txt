THE COST OF WATER HEADROOM IS CONVEX -- BUT LESS SO THAN THE RAW OBJECTIVE SUGGESTS,
AND THE STEEPEST SEGMENT IS MOSTLY A PRICE CAP, NOT A PHYSICAL COST
============================================================================================

REVISION NOTE. An earlier version of this file reported a 117x rise in marginal cost and
said 'China sits at the knee'. Both claims are withdrawn here. Two independent review
findings, which I verified against the model's own cost accounting, cut them down:

  (1) Unserved water is charged at a bare literal 1000 CNY/m3 (solver.py:711). At s = 0.90
      the model stops adapting and simply BUYS the shortfall: 63.7% of the 0.85 -> 0.90
      increment is that penalty, not physical adaptation. My first estimate of the penalty
      share (17%) was wrong -- it priced `slack_detail.csv` volumes by hand, which
      under-captures what the objective charges. `cost_breakdown.csv` carries the model's
      own `slack_penalty` category and is authoritative.
  (2) The knee's LOCATION on the s axis is not identified. The solver sees only
      usable = extractable x (1 - s) x water_multiplier (data_prep.py:452-455), so the
      same physical point sits at s = 0.85 under a 0.20 extractable ceiling and s = 0.925
      under Jin et al. 2022's 0.40. 'China sits at the knee' is a statement about the
      convention, not about China. The curve is therefore reported below against the
      usable share u, which IS identified.

All five runs are on the corrected dry-season basis (digest 9c613b0ddf4b), threads pinned
at 8. Degeneracy floor 0.3277% (95% envelope on a difference, gated seed family n=5).

The upper endpoint divides by the CONTROL'S BOUND, not its incumbent: the maximum of
(OPT_t - OPT_c)/OPT_c over the certified box is attained at OPT_c = LB_c. The previously
published +2.1701% for s = 0.85 understated it; the correct value is +2.1883%.

So the marginal PHYSICAL cost of headroom rises about 42-fold across the range, not 117.
Convexity survives the correction; the headline multiple does not.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT RESOLVED:
  - s = 0.85: effect 1.340%, of which only 5.5% is penalty. Certified interval excludes
    zero. This is the paper's headline and it is 94.5% physical adaptation.
  - s = 0.90: effect 3.994%, but 44.2% of it is the fleet buying shortfall at 1000 CNY/m3.
    Report the physical 2.229%, or re-solve at 10x the penalty before quoting the total.
  - s = 0.56 and 0.70 carry NO penalty at all -- the constraint is met physically -- but
    their effects (0.254%, 0.570%) sit at 0.78x and 1.74x the floor, so the lower half of
    the curve is not resolved and its SHAPE cannot be claimed.

The defensible statement: the cost of water headroom is zero-to-unresolvable while power
keeps more than about 6% of dry-season flow, becomes resolvable and physical at 3%, and
beyond that the model stops adapting and starts paying a price cap. China's published
conventions straddle exactly that range, which is the policy point -- stated on u, where it
is identified, rather than on s, where it is not.
