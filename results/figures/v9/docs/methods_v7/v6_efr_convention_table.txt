  READING IT. The paper solves the tightest convention in the table by a factor of 2.6 against
  China's own law and 2.0 against the closest comparator study, and it offers power
  0.89% of annual runoff. The four-basin / 54% verdict is a property of THAT choice.

  What survives every convention: the Hai's critical reservation share s* does not depend on the
  reservation at all -- s* is defined against the extractable fraction alone -- so the row that
  matters for the scarcity claim is the EXTRACTABLE column. At Richter 0.20 the Hai is negative in
  13 of 20 members. At Jin's 0.40 it is not. The 'capture turns allocation into scarcity' result is
  therefore a statement about a 20% depletion ceiling, and it must be stated with that number
  attached rather than as a general claim about the Hai.
